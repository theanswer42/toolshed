How the app tells you what happened: flash messages, alerts, confirmations and loading.

Result of an action you just took. Shown in the app header, stays until dismissed or replaced.

Confirm dialog

The only modal: a native <dialog> from design.js, used to confirm destructive or hard-to-undo actions.

        data-confirm-title="Delete budget?" data-confirm-label="Delete">Delete</button>
